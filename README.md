# Dashboard (formerly Budget Tracker)

Private, single-user dashboard at **https://amast126.github.io/budget/**. Only the owner's Google account gets in.

- **Home** (`index.html` + `dashboard.js`, source in `src/`): weather for the day (Open-Meteo, no key; location editable, default Dix Hills, NY 11746), a to-do list, money bar, quick add, bills this week, categories running hot, Learning (with a guitar line) and Cooking (with the sourdough starter) cards, Portfolio, and Birthdays. Weather location and to-dos are saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-home` (`src/home-logic.js`, `src/home-cards.jsx`).
  - **Living parts** (`src/pulse-logic.js`, `src/pulse.jsx`): a sky header whose colors follow sunrise and sunset at your weather location (clouds, rain, stars), a line for the time of day (deadlines, bills due, payday, budget pace, calories left, tonight's recipe, tomorrow's weather), and countdown chips (payday, inspection/registration, the next exam or study target, releases marked "on Home" on the Entertainment tab, birthdays in the next week, car payments left). Life rings: Money (at or under budget pace), Body (step goal or a workout), Mind (today's share of your weekly study hours), Home (finish up to 3 to-dos). Streaks for days under your daily budget, active days, food logged, study days and to-do days. A week in review (on Home on Sundays and Mondays, and from the rings card any day) with changes vs the week before. Insights across tabs (dining vs your usual pace, a category running hot, your priciest weekday, payday runway, weight and calorie trends, steps month over month, study pace). A calendar heatmap of spending, steps, study or calories, and small trend lines on the Money, Health and Learning cards. Spending comparisons only count days your imported card data covers. A tennis-weather flag in the weather card.
  - **The sky in the header** (`src/sky.jsx`): drawn on a canvas. Its color follows the sun's real height from your location's sunrise and sunset, through blue hour, sunrise, golden hour and dusk. The sun has a soft bloom and glare; the moon rises and sets by its phase, shows tonight's shape (it can hang pale in a daytime sky), and moonlight silvers the clouds. Stars twinkle, the odd shooting star crosses a clear night, and the Milky Way shows on dark moonless nights. Clouds are generated from noise and lit by marching light through them: cauliflower cumulus with bright tops and grey bases, wispy cirrus, pink and gold undersides at sunrise and sunset. Overcast is a stratocumulus deck that recedes toward the horizon (with a faint town glow at night), rain adds ragged scud racing underneath, storms go dark with a pale horizon, fog drifts in milky banks with a pale sun behind. Birds, a jet drawing a contrail, and a blinking plane at night. Clouds are built a slice per frame and fade in; the text stays readable because the sky behind it is measured and shaded just enough.
  - **Rain and snow in the header** (`src/rain.jsx`): three depth layers of motion-blurred rain plus a few out-of-focus drops, angled by the forecast's wind with gusts, in sweeping curtains. Near drops splash on the bottom edge (with puddle ripples) and off the tops of the countdown chips and weather pill. The header doubles as a window: beads collect, big drops grow, slide down in stick-slip jerks and leave trails. Drizzle, rain, heavy rain and thunderstorms (forked lightning that lights the cloud) each look different; the pointer or a finger works as an umbrella. Snow falls in four depths (fine far flakes, soft flakes, turning six-armed crystals up close, and big out-of-focus flakes), blows with the wind, swirls away from the pointer, settles into drifts on the chips, the weather pill and the bottom edge, and the odd crystal lands on the glass and melts into a bead while frost creeps in from the corners. Both canvases pause off-screen, the rain thins itself on slow devices, and with reduce-motion on each shows one still frame.
  - **Motion** (`src/fx.jsx`, `src/spark.jsx`): numbers count up, bars, rings and chart lines draw in, confetti for wins (a to-do finished, the step goal, all four rings, a week under budget, an exam passed), swipe a to-do right to finish or left to delete, swipe between days on Health, and loading placeholders. All of it turns off with the system's reduce-motion setting. The to-do log per day is kept in the home document (`doneLog`) so streaks survive "Clear done".
  - **Portfolio** (`src/portfolio.jsx`): the holdings from the budget's Portfolio tab with today's price and move (Finnhub, the key in `config.js`, cached 5 minutes), total value and today's change once shares are entered, and one headline feed: news on your own tickers (Finnhub company news, fetched in the browser, so the holdings never appear in this public repo or in `news.json`) plus the energy, quantum and robotics themes from the news job, with a tab for each.
  - **Birthdays** (`src/birthdays*.js*`): type them in or import a `.vcf` (Contacts → select all → Export vCard; birthdays without a year come through too) or a `.csv` with Name and Birthday columns; re-importing updates instead of duplicating. The card shows who's next (with the age they're turning), anyone within a week gets a header chip and moves the card up, and **Add to Calendar (.ics)** makes a calendar file with each birthday repeating yearly and a reminder at 9 AM the day before. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-birthdays`.
- **Look** (`src/theme.js`, `src/glass.css`, `src/lens.jsx`): **Liquid Glass** by default, modeled on iOS 27. The live sky from the Home header is drawn once behind the whole app (`PageSky` in `src/sky.jsx`, at a lower resolution and frame rate, paused while scrolling), and every surface over it is frosted glass: a tint, a blur of what's behind, a lit rim (bright top-left and bottom-right) and a thin darker edge. The sidebar is a glass pane (always open on wide screens) and, in Chrome and Edge, bends what's behind it at its edges with an SVG displacement filter (Safari can't do that on the web, so there it stays frosted). The page's own background follows the sky's top color (so the status bar strip and Safari's bars match it), the sky layer runs taller than the screen so it reaches under Safari's bottom bar, and as a Home Screen app the sky continues behind the status bar. Light or dark follows the device. Settings → Appearance has iOS 27's transparency slider (Clear to Tinted) and **Classic**, the original paper-and-green look; both are remembered per device. The Budget tab sits on the same pane of glass: `budget.html` drops its page background when shown inside the dashboard, and in dark mode the dashboard inverts the frame's colors (the budget module's own colors can't be restyled from outside). `index.html` sets the look before the first paint so there's no flash.
- **Navigation**: on wide screens the sidebar is always there; on phones a small button at the bottom left (it names the tab you're on) opens it as a drawer with every tab and Settings. It closes when you pick a tab, tap outside, swipe it left or press Escape.
- **News**: its own tab with US politics (AP, Reuters), NYC politics (Gothamist, THE CITY, City & State, Politico New York, amNY, Daily News, plus Mamdani coverage), Tech & AI (The Verge, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, Wired, Reuters tech), Reddit (r/popular), Pop culture (Variety, THR, Vulture, EW), Music (Pitchfork, Billboard, Stereogum, Rolling Stone, Guitar World) and Markets (energy, quantum computing and robotics, tagged by theme). The job also writes Gaming and Marvel sections for the Entertainment tab. Sources are set at the top of `scripts/fetch-news.mjs`.
- **Entertainment** (`src/fun.jsx`, `src/fun-logic.js`): a live countdown to the featured release (GTA VI, Nov 19, 2026, with the latest Rockstar newswire posts and trailers from the GTA 6 site's `/gta6/news.json`), everything else coming up (MW4 Oct 23, VisionQuest Oct 14, Avengers: Doomsday Dec 18; add your own, star one to feature it, and pick which count down on Home), Now playing with challenge counters (Black Ops 7 Zombies' Totenreich Cursed challenges and the Rex Infernus Easter egg to start, plus TFT and Pokémon GO), the **Road to Doomsday** watch list (91 titles: MCU Phases 1–6, Fox's X-Men and Deadpool films, the pre-MCU Spider-Man films and Marvel's Netflix shows, grouped, each Watched, Skipped or still to watch, with a pace to finish by Dec 18; "Key" marks the titles on NME's pre-Doomsday list as a hint), and Gaming and Marvel news. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-fun`.
- **Budget** (`budget.html` + `app.js`): the original budget tracker, unchanged, shown inside the app. Its source is not in this repo; `app.js` is the prebuilt bundle.
- **Learning** (`src/learning*.js*`): Cloud & AI certification roadmap with links, descriptions and impact, progress tracking (status, exam dates, study hours, renewals). Cert details live in `src/learning-catalog.js`; progress is saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-learning`.
  - **Guitar** (`src/guitar.jsx`, `src/guitar-logic.js`), the second section of Learning (`#/learning?guitar`): a practice timer that keeps running while you use the rest of the app, quick logs, a daily goal, the streak and the week; where you are in JustinGuitar's course (grade, module, the lesson you're on; "Finished module" moves you on); one-minute chord changes with your best and trend per pair; songs (want to learn, learning, can play); a tuner (the microphone, pitch found in the browser, nothing recorded) and a metronome with tap tempo. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-guitar`.
- **Cooking** (`src/cooking*.js*`, `src/ingredients.mjs`): kitchen list (fridge, freezer, pantry, spices, with a "running low" flag), grocery list, and recipes matched against what you have. "Finish shop" logs the total under Groceries in the budget and moves bought items into the kitchen. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-cooking`.
  - **Sourdough corner** (`src/sourdough.jsx`, `src/sourdough-logic.js`), the second section of Cooking (`#/cooking?sourdough`): the starter (feedings, counter or fridge, when it should peak from the feed ratio and kitchen temperature), a bake planner that works back from when you want bread out of the oven (levain, autolyse, bulk with folds, shape, cold proof, preheat, bake; the cold proof and levain ratio flex to keep hands-on steps between 7 AM and 11 PM), a dough calculator in baker's percentages, and a bake log with ratings. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-sourdough`.
- **Auto** (`src/auto.jsx`, `src/auto-logic.js`): the 2021 Altima SL AWD. Deadlines (NYS inspection, registration, insurance renewal), maintenance on Nissan's schedule (the schedule, warranty and picture come from a per-car profile, and inspection and registration rules from a per-state table, so the demo's Tesla in Washington works too) with a service log (costs can go straight into Gas & Auto), mileage, warranty, NHTSA recalls, and costs read from the budget (car payment, Geico, Gas & Auto). A Home card shows what's due. Saved in `trackers/alec-tracker-auto`.
- **Health** (`src/health.jsx`, `src/health-logic.js`, `src/food-api.js`): daily calories and macros against a target (Mifflin–St Jeor from sex, age, height, weight and activity, or your own numbers), a food log by meal with USDA FoodData Central search, barcode lookup (Open Food Facts, then USDA) by camera, photo or number, recent foods, quick add, and copy-yesterday; weight with a 7-day trend chart; steps and workouts; a 7-day summary; a Home card. Budget Bytes recipes on the Cooking tab can be logged by the serving. Profile, weigh-ins and the food library are in `trackers/alec-tracker-health`; each year's days are in `trackers/alec-tracker-health-<year>`. Barcode reading on iPhone uses ZXing (`vendor/zxing-0.23.0.min.js`, Apache-2.0, loaded only when scanning).
- **Apple Health** (`src/apple-health.js`, `src/hk-logic.js`, `src/health-hk.jsx`, `src/chart-kit.jsx`): import the Health app's export.zip (Health → your picture → Export All Health Data) from the Apple Health card on the Health tab. The file is read in the browser (zip and XML streamed, nothing uploaded) and boiled down to daily numbers: steps and distance (iPhone and Watch combined hour by hour so nothing is counted twice), activity rings, workouts with routes, resting/walking heart rate, HRV, daily heart-rate range, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, VO₂ max, heart-rate recovery, ECGs (128 samples a second), sleep with stages (one source per night, Watch first), walking speed/step length/double support/asymmetry/steadiness, stair speed, six-minute walk, headphone and environmental sound levels, the audiogram, scale body composition, handwashing and mindfulness. It adds the Activity, Heart, Sleep, Body and Hearing views, the day's rings, vitals and Watch workouts on Today, Apple steps in the week and on Home, and a Watch-based calorie check in Targets. Old weigh-ins are added to your weight log (ones far from your usual weight are skipped as typos), and sex, birthday (for age) and height fill any blanks. Stored separately from what you type, so re-importing never touches your log: `trackers/alec-tracker-health-hk` (monthly averages, workouts, readings), `-health-hk-<year>` (days), `-health-hk-ecg` and `-health-hk-routes` (loaded only when opened). Tests use a made-up export from `test/make-health-export.mjs`; no real health data is in this repo.
- **Demo mode** (`src/demo.js`, `src/demo-data.js`, `src/demo-flag.js`, `budget-demo.html`): open `…/budget/?demo`, tap **Try the demo** on the sign-in screen, or use **Switch to demo mode** in Settings. **Copy the demo link** in Settings copies `…/budget/demo.html`, the link to share: Messages, Slack and other apps show a designed preview card for it (`demo-preview.jpg`, from its Open Graph tags), and tapping it goes straight into the demo. The demo shows the whole app with sample data for a made-up person whose life and money are nothing like yours: Jordan Rivera, a product lead in Seattle on about $300k a year, with a condo mortgage, a Tesla Model Y on a loan, a dog, a brokerage account, about eight months of budget history, a food log, a year of Apple Health data (runs around Green Lake, summer rides, sleep, an ECG), an AI study plan of its own and a stocked kitchen. No sign-in is needed and Firebase is never started, so nothing touches the real account. The sample is generated relative to today with the app's own functions and kept in this browser under `demo:` keys; changes stay until **Reset** (in the demo bar or Settings), and **Exit demo** (or **Back to my dashboard** if you came from Settings) returns to the normal page. Bumping `VERSION` in `src/demo.js` replaces older samples on the next visit. The Budget tab runs the budget module in its local mode on the same sample from `budget-demo.html`, a separate page so a cached copy of the real one can never stand in. Weather is live for Seattle. Personal touches stay out of the demo: no GTA VI countdown, running weather instead of tennis, and the Learning tab shows the demo person's plan with reasons written for them. The demo person also has their own games (Mario Kart World, Baldur's Gate 3, Balatro) and countdown (Dune: Part Three), guitar practice, a sourdough starter and birthdays.
- **Recipes** (`recipes.json`, written weekly by `scripts/fetch-recipes.mjs`): popular Budget Bytes recipes (25+ ratings, 4.3+ stars) with cost per serving and ingredient names, plus 12 picks a week that don't repeat for 12 weeks. Only titles, links, photos, costs and ingredients are kept; the cooking steps stay on budgetbytes.com.

Both read and write the same Firestore document (`trackers/alec-tracker`), so a quick add or a bill tick on Home appears in the budget within a second, and the other way round.

## Access

Access is enforced twice: `config.js` (the app refuses other accounts) and the Firestore rules in the Firebase console (the data refuses other accounts). The rules allow only the owner's verified email to read or write `trackers/*`.

## Build and test

```bash
npm install
./build.sh
BUDGET_EXPORT=/path/to/budget-tracker-export.json RECIPES_FIXTURE=/path/to/recipes.json EAN_IMAGE=/path/to/barcode.png node test/run.mjs shots/
```

## Deploy

1. Change files in `src/` (upload at `https://github.com/amast126/budget/upload/main/src`, or edit in the GitHub web editor) and commit.
2. The **Build dashboard** workflow rebuilds `dashboard.js` and bumps the version in `index.html` automatically (Actions tab).
3. Wait 1–3 minutes for Pages, reload, and check the build number in Settings.

`config.js` and the budget module (`budget.html`, `budget-demo.html`, `app.js`) are not built: bump their `?v=` by hand if they ever change (in both budget pages).
